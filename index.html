Between 2021 and 2026, Thimmarajupeta underwent a transformative shift. Through collaborative community efforts, structural modernization, and enhanced connectivity, our village established a new benchmark for rural development in Andhra Pradesh.

Every initiative—from agricultural water management to digital literacy—was designed to empower the next generation while preserving our rich cultural identity.

2021 Phase One

Foundational Planning & Drainage Systems

Digital Integration & Sustainable Future

Completed solar street lighting installation, deployed high-speed public Wi-Fi zones, and finalized digital documentation for community welfare programs.
