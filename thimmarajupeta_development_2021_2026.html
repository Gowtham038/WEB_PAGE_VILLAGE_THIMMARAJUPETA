Core Focus Areas

Pillars of Transformation

Targeted civic investments engineered to elevate everyday quality of life in Thimmarajupeta.

01 / Connectivity

Roads & Storm Drainage

Construction of reinforced Cement Concrete (CC) internal streets, approach road widening, and covered storm-water drains to eliminate monsoon waterlogging.

02 / Public Welfare

Education & Healthcare

Modernized classrooms, clean sanitation blocks, compound walls for village schools, and strengthened primary health wellness facilities for families and elders.

03 / Sustainability

Water & Green Spaces

Rejuvenation of village ponds, purified RO drinking water plants, avenue plantation drives, and dedicated community gathering parks.

Chronicle of Growth

2021 – 2026 Roadmap

Year-by-year execution milestones marking steady infrastructure upgrades across all wards.

2021 – 2022

Foundation & Civic Audit

Ward-level infrastructure mapping, initial drainage clearance, and Phase-1 LED street lighting installation.

2022 – 2023

School & Water Upgrades

Renovation of primary school buildings, digital classroom setup, and purified RO water plant commissioning.

2023 – 2024

CC Roads Expansion

Major laying of cement concrete roads connecting main colonies and agricultural approach pathways.

2024 – 2025

Community & Sanitation

Solid waste management sheds, community hall beautification, and solar high-mast junction lights.

2025 – 2026

Smart Village Vision

Full-coverage tap water grid, green park corridors, and digital citizen service documentation.
